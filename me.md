A dummy text is a temporary placeholder of nonsensical or scrambled words used in graphic design, web development, and publishing to fill a page or layout before the actual final copy is ready.

Why Use Dummy Text?
Prevents Distraction: It lets clients and designers focus on visual elements—such as font size, color, spacing, and page structure—without getting side-tracked by reading actual content.
Simulates Real Copy: It mimics the natural distribution of letters and word lengths found in actual human language.
Drafting Layouts: It allows print and digital templates to be designed and tested independently of the final writing.
